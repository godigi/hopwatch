# Hopwatch Visual Tour & Screenshots

Hopwatch is designed for macOS Sonoma and Sequoia, offering both a compact menu bar companion and a detailed multi-tab diagnostic window. It natively supports macOS system appearances in both Dark and Light modes.

---

## 1. Menu Bar Dropdown
Instant telemetry at your fingertips. Click the menu bar icon anytime to inspect current latency, Wi-Fi link speed, gateway status, and root cause diagnosis without interrupting your workflow.
